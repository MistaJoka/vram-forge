// read of the market

2026 has been a memory-shortage year. DRAM/NAND fabs shifted to HBM for datacenters, so consumer DDR5, SSDs and VRAM-heavy GPUs rose together. Nvidia kept MSRPs unchanged; the increase shows up as street-price markup. The highest-VRAM consumer parts (5090, used 3090/4090, 128GB unified boxes) rose the most because AI buyers compete for them. Metric that matters: $/GB of VRAM at your target model size, then bandwidth.
